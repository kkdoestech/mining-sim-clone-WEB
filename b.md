### 1. Early Game & Unlocking the Dwarven Mines

* **Fast-Track to Mining 12 [[01:27](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D87)]:** Buy sticks and gold in the Hub to craft a Golden Shovel, enchant it with Efficiency IV, and mine sand in the Desert Settlement until reaching Mining Level 12.
* **Accessing the Dwarven Mines [[02:16](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D136)]:** Head through the Gold Mines into the Deep Caverns down to the Obsidian Sanctuary. Talk to Rhys and turn in 30 of any enchanted ore (e.g., purchased directly from the Bazaar) to unlock the Dwarven Mines.

### 2. Dwarven Mines & The Push to HOTM 7

* **Starter Gear & Commissions [[03:17](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D197)]:** Purchase the Fractured Mithril Pickaxe from Bubu. Talk to the King in the Royal Palace [[03:35](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D215)] to unlock Commissions and access the Heart of the Mountain (HOTM) skill tree.
* **Early Equipment Progression:**
* **HOTM 2 [[04:38](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D278)]:** Unlock the Forge to craft equipment. Get the **Mithril Drill SX-R226** (+20% Mithril Powder), equip a **Mithril Golem Pet** [[04:59](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D299)], and wear a full set of **Glacite Armor** with Royal-reforged Mithril equipment.
* **HOTM 3 [[05:31](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D331)]:** Upgrade to the **Mithril Drill SX-R326** (+40% Mithril Powder) and craft Titanium Equipment at the Forge.


* **Crucial QoL Shards & Perks:**
* Buy the **Troglobite Shard** (+10% HOTM XP) [[06:21](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D381)], Miner Zombie Shard (+30 Mining Speed), and Star Sentry Shard (+10% Mithril Powder).
* Prioritize maxing **Efficient Miner** first [[07:42](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D462)] for Mining Spread to break multiple Mithril blocks per hit. Use **Pickobulus** [[08:06](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D486)] as your primary pickaxe ability to blast blocks quickly during commissions.
* Get an **Aspect of the End (AOTE)** or **Aspect of the Void (AOTV)** [[09:16](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D556)] for rapid navigation.


* **Mining Events [[10:44](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D644)]:** Always participate in Dwarven Mines events (Raffles, Goblin Raids, and Mithril Gourmand) to farm Mithril Powder rapidly. Before an event concludes, swap to your Mithril Drill and ensure your Mithril Golem pet is active to earn bonus powder [[13:46](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D826)].

### 3. Crystal Hollows & Gemstone Powder Grinding

* **Powder Grinding Priority [[14:34](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D874)]:** Reaching HOTM 7 unlocks Gemstone Powder farming in the Crystal Hollows by opening treasure chests in Hard Stone.
* **Optimizing Chests:**
* Max out **Great Explorer** [[14:50](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D890)] to spawn chests frequently and bypass lockpicking entirely. Upgrade Great Explorer in increments of 5 to reduce lock requirements, dumping intermediate powder into **Powder Buff** [[16:52](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1012)].
* Max out **Fortunate Mineman** and **Speedy Mineman** next [[19:24](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1164)].
* Powder grind during **2x Powder Events** [[16:15](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D975)] in the **Precursor Ruins** [[18:17](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1097)] using Mineral Armor (or Glossy Mineral) so chests drop valuable Automaton Parts for passive profit.


* **Nucleus Runs & Commissions:**
* Crystal Hollows commissions are slow and heavily RNG-dependent—**stick to Dwarven Mines commissions** to level HOTM [[21:30](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1290)].
* Complete Nucleus Runs [[19:47](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1187)] up to 50 times solely for SkyBlock XP or a chance at the rare Divan’s Alloy [[21:05](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1265)].

### 4. Glacite Tunnels & Reaching HOTM 10

* **Unlocking Base Camp [[22:16](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1336)]:** Forge a **Secret Railroad Pass** and hand it to a Ticket Master to unlock the Dwarven Base Camp (`/warp glacite`).
* **Gear Baseline [[23:48](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1428)]:**
* Full **Divan Armor** (Jaded reforge, slotted gemstones).
* Near-maxed **DRX-655 Drill** (with Powder Coating).
* **Scatha** or **Bal Pet**, and Dwarven Handwarmers.


* **Glacite Powder & Fossil Excavation [[25:16](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1516)]:** Glacite Powder is gained via Glacite commissions, mining, or donating fossils found via Suspicious Scrap at the Fossil Excavator (best donated during 2x powder events).
* **Mine Shafts & Vanguard Corpses [[26:54](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1614)]:**
* Mining blocks in the tunnels has a chance to spawn Mine Shaft portals. Mobs inside only take damage from drills [[28:31](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1711)].
* Loot corpses using forged keys. Vanguard Mine Shafts contain a Vanguard Corpse [[28:53](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1733)] (unlocked via Skeleton Key) which can drop the **Shattered Locket** for the **Pendant of Divan**.
* Talk to **Grandpa Wolf** [[31:01](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1861)] to track corpse milestones and use the Frozen Corpse RNG meter to guarantee a Shattered Locket over time.


* **Leveling to HOTM 10 [[33:05](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D1985)]:** Glacite Tunnel commissions award massive amounts of HOTM XP, making the 7–10 grind substantially faster than 1–7.
* **Remote Turn-ins [[33:58](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D2038)]:** Glacite Tunnels lack emissaries, making the **Royal Pigeon** (unlocked at 500 commissions) essential for claiming commissions remotely without leaving your mining spot.

### 5. Final Key Tips

* Equip the **Glacite Golem Pet** (+20% Glacite Powder) [[35:06](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D2106)] and look for the Stalagmite attribute (+10% Glacite Powder).
* Mining speed is server-tick and ping-dependent [[35:26](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D2126)], so stable low ping provides smoother block breaks.
* Speak to **Fragulus** [[35:45](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DG3QQLLkJQCM%26t%3D2145)] in the tunnels to review block classifications and understand how block spread applies across ores versus gemstones.
